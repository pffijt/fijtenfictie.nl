**Irina Koriazova** is an Amsterdam-based storyteller. Born in a provincial city in Russia, she never thought she would leave. And yet here she is, trying to make sense of her life through telling stories. She has a dry sense of humour and she likes to turn things upside down: a huge scary monster becomes worthy of love, and a victim turns into a protagonist. Irina often hosts and performs at many storytelling venues and events all over the Netherlands.

**Christos Sougk** comes from Greece, where they eventually had to migrate due to allergic reactions to sunlight. They advocate for the representation of people with nasal voices and broken English in popular culture. While other comics strive for relatability, Christos leaves audiences either in stitches or utterly confused, with an absurd comedic style that pushes the boundaries of good taste and decency. When a comedian of such caliber is on the bill, only one thing is certain: you are in for a fully realized assault on the senses, with an impact just as memorable as a neatly executed amputation.
